Friends

People I know with corners of the web worth visiting.

It turned out to be the most useful thing I did there — not because of the meetings, but because of who showed up to them. Five of the people below are co-authors with me on a paper about AI text detectors. Three were on the coding subteam that built the solar car dashboard. The club is still running, now as a Hack Club chapter, several years after all of us left.
