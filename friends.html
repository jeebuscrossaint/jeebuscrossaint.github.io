Friends.

People I know with corners of the web worth visiting.

Most of them I met through the coding club I started at FAU High School in 2023. It turned out to be the most useful thing I did there — not because of the meetings, but because of who showed up to them.

Five of the people below are co-authors with me on a paper about AI text detectors. Three were on the coding subteam that built the solar car dashboard that took second in its division at the Solar Car Challenge. The club is still running, now as a Hack Club chapter, several years after all of us left.
